## Legal conclusion after confirmation

Under Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC, a micro-enterprise has fewer than 10 annual work units and turnover and/or balance-sheet total not above EUR 2 million. A small enterprise has fewer than 50 annual work units and turnover and/or balance-sheet total not above EUR 10 million. Partner and linked enterprise data must be considered.

If the operator confirms ICEPUNK meets either threshold, DSA Article 19 excludes the Section 3 online-platform obligations, except Article 24(3), while the hosting-service obligations in DSA Articles 16 and 17 still apply. ICEPUNK nevertheless provides an appeal route voluntarily.

## Operational commitments

- Maintain the Article 16 notice route and Article 17 statements of reasons.
- Provide average monthly active-recipient information if requested by the Digital Services Coordinator or Commission under Article 24(3).

- If the small/micro status is lost, obtain legal advice and implement the applicable Section 3 obligations before relying on the exemption again.
