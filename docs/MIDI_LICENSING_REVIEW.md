# MIDI licensing review

## Finding: release blocker — rights chain is not evidenced

The repository contains `analysis_output/midi_analysis.json`, which references 51 source paths such as `midi_dataset\\midi_1.mid`. The source MIDI corpus and its provenance, licences, contributor agreements and any attribution/redistribution restrictions are not present in the repository. Therefore iCEPUNK cannot currently substantiate a blanket statement that Factory outputs are royalty-free, commercially usable, exclusive, original, or free of third-party claims.

Custom uploads and saved datasets are also a separate risk: the generator can derive patterns from user-provided MIDI. A platform cannot grant a licence in material it does not own or have authority to sublicense.

## Required evidence before any commercial/royalty-free claim

1. Asset register for every Factory source: supplier/contributor, immutable source reference, licence text, acquisition date, territory, commercial use, derivative/ML-analysis permission, attribution and redistribution requirements.
2. Written contributor/contractor assignment or licence confirming iCEPUNK may analyse, generate from, host and sublicense outputs as proposed.
3. Legal review of copyright/database/neighbouring-rights exposure, similarity/derivative-output policy and wording for the intended territories.
4. Product decision: licence scope, commercial use, attribution, exclusivity, prohibited uses, disclaimer, indemnity approach, termination and version/change process.
5. Community uploader terms: affirmative rights confirmation, prohibited-content rules, repeat-infringer/moderation process, notice-and-action contact, appeal process and evidence preservation.

## Current publishing decision

The operator has instructed that Factory-only outputs are offered under a royalty-free commercial-use policy, while Custom-source outputs remain the uploader's responsibility. That policy is now expressed in the Terms. This instruction is a product decision, **not** source-chain evidence: items 1–4 remain required before presenting the claim in paid advertising or representing it as legally reviewed.
