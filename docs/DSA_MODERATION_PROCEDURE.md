# DSA notice and moderation procedure

**Owner:** Danylo Nikulin
**Service:** ICEPUNK / icepunk.app
**Effective:** 2026-11-14
**Review:** annually and on a material product or ownership change

## Scope

ICEPUNK stores user uploads and makes selected user MIDI packs visible in its Community feed. It therefore operates a notice-and-action process for alleged illegal content and content that breaches its Terms.

## Notice intake

The public **Report** action provides two routes:

- **Formal DSA notice:** exact content identifier, category, optional explanation, reporter name, contact email, and an affirmative good-faith declaration. The identifier is the location of the reported content in the ICEPUNK service.
- **Anonymous community report:** available where a reporter does not want to identify themselves. It is reviewed using the same safety process, but ICEPUNK cannot send a decision to an anonymous reporter.

The service returns a report reference immediately. For a formal notice, it also sends a receipt to the submitted email address. Reports are rate-limited and recorded with the original content identifier, type, category, timing and submission route.

## Triage and decisions

1. Review the report against applicable law and the ICEPUNK Terms. Urgent illegality or safety reports take priority.
2. Record the factual basis, the relevant legal or Terms basis, and the action: no restriction, hide, or restore.
3. Do not include a reporter's identity or private internal notes in notices to the author.
4. For a resolved report, send the author and, if available, the reporter a statement of the decision, its reason and the available redress route.
5. A hide action makes the material private at the database level. The author cannot republish it while that restriction remains active.

## Statement of reasons template

Each restriction decision must state:

- reported content ID/type and the restriction applied;
- factual circumstances considered;
- applicable legal ground or ICEPUNK Terms rule;
- whether the decision followed a report or own-initiative review;
- how the affected author can request review through `/moderation`.

## Review and appeal

The affected author can submit a free electronic appeal from the moderation page. A moderator reviews it without undue delay, records the outcome, and sends the outcome and reasons. ICEPUNK provides this process voluntarily while its DSA small/micro-enterprise assessment remains valid.

## Retention and access

Only personnel who need the record to moderate, address a complaint, protect the service or meet legal obligations may access it. Retention follows the published privacy notice; records are deleted or anonymised when no longer needed, subject to statutory retention duties.
